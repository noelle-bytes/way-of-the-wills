The Way

What is the Way of the Wills, in a sentence or two?

The Way(s) of the Will(s) is the idea that some objects have wills, recursive loops that modify their own functions to maintain themselves, and that those wills affect their environment in much the same way desire paths wear trails, or rain carves rivers.

What counts as a will, and what doesn't?

Anything that modifies its own functions to maintain itself has a will. The process is time bound. A will cannot exist without time.

Why does a flame have a will when a magnet doesn't?

A magnet is a flag blowing in the wind. It doesn't self-reinforce, it changes direction based on the Earth's wills, not its own. A magnet will decay if left alone, a flame will grow, change and only die when it cannot find the fuel or environment to maintain itself.

How is a will different from awareness?

Awareness is when wills construct in a way that builds a self referencing model. A will can exist without awareness because a will is merely the force that maintains itself, it doesn't need a self referencing model to do that.

Where did the idea come from for you?

I was looking into awareness, and found the issue of collective intelligence interesting to work out, and then I was looking into various post-Christian organizations and theologies, and I realized no one had created exactly the toolbox I wanted, and the closest failed to explain why things happened and what I should do about them. A lot of them have no backbone, or the backbone they have is rooted in dogma and falsehoods.

So I made my own toolbox based on science.

The slime mold is a great example of awareness and wills combining and dividing, and to be fair, we think slime molds are irl flubber, and who doesn't like flubber?

Is it the will of the way, or the way of the wills?

Both. I say both. They both mean different things, but ultimately it doesn't matter, but the way of the wills is generally the idea, and the will of the way describes the concept as the desire path effect.

How It Works

How do small wills add up to bigger ones?

Much in the same way that functions/algorithms combine and conflict to create the maintained loop of lesser wills, lesser wills combine and conflict to create regular wills. Likewise, wills can combine and clash to create the loose collectives that are greater wills. Depending on how you classify us as either regular wills or greater wills, there are greater wills that we are apart of. Groups of humans form collectives in many different ways, like a flock of birds or sheep, we form metaphysical collectives based on conscious and unconscious beliefs and actions.

These greater and lesser wills are often attributed to spirits or gods or God in the past, but all of these things, from the air you breathe, to morality itself are effects of the way of the wills, effects of these collectives of wills.

Can one will be part of several bigger wills at once?

Yes. You are part of countless wills and made of countless wills that combined to make you. Cancer is what happens when the will of a cell becomes misaligned with the rest of your cells. Michael Levin calls it a cell with a smaller self.

What decides whether a will becomes aware?

If the wills function as a model of itself and make decisions based on that model.

Why do speed and size matter?

Because the difference between a whole and a group is the speed at which that group communicates. If signals cross the whole faster than any one of its parts makes decisions, it can be treated as a single will. If the communication is slow enough that every part makes decisions apart from the whole, then we can treat it as a collective.

Why is a person aware when a country isn't?

The country is too spread apart and communicates too slowly to be aware more than a forest is.

How do wills talk to each other through the world around them?

Through direct communication and indirectly based on the changes they make to their environment and other wills.

What's the difference between a fire spreading and a slime mold searching?

One is unable to create a self referencing model, the other can. The slime mold leaves a trail of slime and uses it to remember where it's been.

Which parts are proven science, and which parts are your own?
